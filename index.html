Understanding Taiwan's Official Halal Certification System

Taiwan's Tourism Administration collaborates closely with the Chinese Muslim Association (CMA) and the Taiwan Halal Integrity Development Association (THIDA) to establish clear, trustworthy certification tiers.

1. Halal Restaurant (HR)
Owned & Operated by Muslims

Entire premises and kitchen are strictly 100% Halal. No pork, pork derivatives, or alcoholic beverages on the premises.

2. Muslim-Friendly Restaurant (MFR)
Non-Muslim Owned, Segregated Kitchen

Certified dedicated Halal cooking equipment, separate tableware, certified halal meat suppliers, and no pork or alcohol in certified dishes.

3. Muslim-Friendly Tourism (MFT)
Hotels & Resorts

Rooms feature Qibla compasses, prayer rugs, bidet sprays or washlets, no alcohol in minibar, and certified Halal breakfast buffet or set meals.
